# Onboarding Image Prompts

Style shared across all cards: isometric projection, geometric stylized shapes like Scandinavian wooden toys. Dark navy palette (#0B0E14 base, #121620 structures). Minimal soft glow effects at light sources allowed. Subtle grain texture. No text, no people. Warm amber light from upper-left. PNG with alpha channel, transparent background.

Isometric miniature harbour scene at dusk, viewed from above at 30-degree angle. A small Nordic harbour. One warehouse building with an open door emitting a warm teal glow (#3ECFB4). A wooden dock extends into calm, dark water. A single small boat approaches the dock. A lighthouse on a rocky outcrop, its lamp emitting a warm amber glow. Tiny geometric bollards on the dock. Calm harbour water with subtle teal ripple lines. 5-7 objects max. Transparent background.
